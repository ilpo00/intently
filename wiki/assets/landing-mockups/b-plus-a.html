What I deliberately did NOT take from A, and why. A's centre layout is its signature — but it can't coexist with the side-by-side living preview. Centring B means deleting the preview to make room, which trades away the exact thing that makes B land. So B stays two-pane; we borrow A's behaviour, not its geometry. The only other change is cosmetic: the input is a hair larger (A's confidence) — easy to revert if it reads as too big. If any of this feels like one addition too many, the promise line is the first thing I'd cut.
